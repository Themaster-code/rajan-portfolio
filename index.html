I'm Rajan Deshmukh, a YouTube Thumbnail Designer. My work uses an understanding of viewer psychology to create visuals that make people stop scrolling and click.

Rather than relying on excessive effects, I focus on cinematic realism, facial emotions with eye contact, visual hierarchy, and strong composition.

Every design is created with one purpose: Increase Click-Through Rate while maintaining authenticity.
