Editing Philosophy

I believe that editing a video gives us access to the entire mindset of the person watching it. And that brings two things worth looking at.

Most people who edit videos do not necessarily do it with complete awareness of what they are doing; they work for money. Because of this, their own superstitions and conditioning remain with them. As a result, an edit can be used to manipulate people—to fool them further and push them even deeper into the same pit they are already in. The intention becomes to earn money so that our own lives can improve, while the damage happens on both sides.

The editor’s life does not necessarily become better after receiving the money, because there is still no self-knowledge, no realization of the self. And the people whose lives have been negatively influenced or destroyed have already been harmed. When the intention itself is wrong, there is ultimately nothing meaningful left standing.

If I get the opportunity to edit videos for Acharya Prashant, however, there will be something beyond just earning from the work. There will also be a sense of peace—the peace that one wants to have in life. I will know that I am doing the work in the right direction, that I am not merely exploiting someone’s beliefs, but using the edit to help bring them towards the right thing.

At the same time, this creates a very strong opportunity to work with the game of audience retention in a meaningful way. Whether through audio, color correction, sound effects, B-roll, or storytelling, every element can be used from the right center and with the right intention.

That is why I believe it would be a very meaningful position to get the opportunity to edit Acharya Ji’s videos, so that they can reach the right people. At the same time, it would be necessary to remain updated with what is happening in the current visual and media landscape. This means continuously observing the fonts used in movies and series, understanding the designs and visual language that are currently working in the market, and staying informed about what is being created and consumed at a subconscious level so that the editing remains relevant and connected with the present audience.
